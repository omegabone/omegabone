## Why verbatim

These are real students' words used in marketing. Transcripts are captured verbatim —
filler words trimmed, nothing else. Never paraphrase a quote into a stronger claim than the
student made, and never generate a quote that was not said. Machine transcription errors are
corrected where clear; genuinely unclear audio is marked `[unclear]` rather than guessed at.

### 4. What happened the first time you buzzed through an entire song? What did you notice in your body?

| Time | |
|---|---|
| 02:57 | Before, I knew about this technique, but I was not realizing what it actually does. When you buzz you actually create the sound in the necessary part of your mouth, because the resonance happens in a very specific place. |
| 03:23 | And when we speak, and we have never practiced voice with a vocal coach, we don't know that. But there is a specific point where this resonance happens. And when we buzz, we reach this point, and we can reach very high notes, even ones we cannot do when we are singing. But when we are singing it's actually easier to reach them after buzzing. So it just makes it easier to understand how to navigate your voice. |

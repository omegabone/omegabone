Timeline of Events

  1. Prior to April 2024

    Supervision and music program responsibilities.

    I developed the music magnet curriculum, obtained grants and music equipment, organized performances, and supervised the Music Teaching Assistant, Nico Craig. As his supervisor, I was responsible for setting work expectations and addressing lateness, classroom conduct, phone use, incomplete assignments, and access to the music rooms.

  2. April 11, 2024

    Classroom attire incident.

    Nico removed an outer shirt and exposed his breasts. I told him to put his shirt back on because we were working in a school setting. In the later HR/EOS process, Ms. Espinoza confirmed the exposure. The corresponding written communication is absent from the disclosure, but his complaint confirms that he showed me his nipples.

  3. April 15, 2024

    Ellen Craig's text messages and witness Demonte.

    At 9:05 a.m., I asked whether “Chicklette” was going to the assembly, because Demonte didn’t want to miss it. Her later messages included “Will be sending your favorite back momentarily” and “He's on his way back.” “Chicklette” referred to Demonte, not Nico. These messages corroborate Demonte's presence that day. I was in a good mood as I liked how smart Demonte is. The District did not interview him, despite its suggestion that no student witnessed the incident.

    PNGEllen Craig texts: "Will be sending your favorite back momentarily" / "He's on his way back"
  4. April 15, 2024

    Nico's threat and my request for assistance.

    Nico arrived very late after the email exchange with his mom, and was in a bad mood. When I instructed him about his work, he warned me, “Watch out!” I asked what he meant and whether he intended to get me fired as Mr. Handy, the former fifth-grade substitute, had been removed. I believed Ellen Craig had been involved in Mr. Handy's removal. I suggested Nico take a break and see his mother, but he refused. Demonte and I went to Principal Lorraine Persons' office. I reported the threat and requested Nico's removal from my Music Magnet assignment because his conduct was becoming more than I could manage. Nico arrived at the office approximately thirty minutes later; when I left, he asked where I was going, and I rolled my eyes and left.

  5. April 15–16, 2024

    My supervisory email and Persons' intervention.

    I prepared “Moving Forward,” an email setting professional expectations for Nico's attendance, classroom duties, communication, attire, documentation, and deadlines, and copied Persons. She had texted about a meeting around 1 p.m. On April 15 at 7:06 p.m., she wrote, “You probably should let me see the email before you send it to Niko.” I sent the email later that night, around 10 p.m., without waiting for her approval. At 6:30 a.m. on April 16 I confirmed to her, “I sent it last night and hopefully he'll be in on time to meet this morning before classes start.” I believe Persons was improperly intervening in my responsibility to supervise the assistant.

  6. April 16, 2024

    Complaint notice and Joyce's report.

    One day after my supervisory email, Persons issued notice naming me as the respondent in Nico's discrimination and harassment complaint. She undertook an investigative role despite her direct involvement in the supervisory dispute, which I believe compromised the process. She made no mention of any physical contact between Nico and I. Joyce also reported Nico's conduct to me that day. The District did not interview Joyce about her firsthand report.

    PDFPersons notice naming me respondent in Nico's complaint
  7. April 19, 2024

    Nico's response and the resulting investigation.

    I received Nico's counter-complaint rather than an acknowledgment of the “Moving Forward” expectations. He accused me of berating and yelling at him, creating a hostile work environment, and imposing requirements that he claimed violated his contract. He also alleged I said I felt like taking an AR-15 and “letting it rip” on students. That I hit him in front of Ms TT. I deny those allegations. Nico claimed to have privately confided his transgender identity to me, although he had publicly discussed his transition in a November 2021 FOX Soul interview. I believe Persons worked with Nico and Ellen Craig to prepare or facilitate this counter-complaint during April 15–19. I contend it became the basis for later HR/EOS and disciplinary proceedings while my own reports were not fairly addressed. I have stated that HR/EOS deemed Nico's accusations hearsay and unfounded, but that written determination is absent from the disclosure. After Nico's removal from my classroom, he continued to come near it despite his reassignment; I continued reporting that conduct.

    PDFNico Craig's complaint, the foundation of the case
  8. May 2024

    Safety reporting.

    I continued reporting concerns regarding student safety and Nico's interactions with students. A May 23, 2024 communication from Dr. Sample addressed reporting to DCFS or police regarding a student's account. My reports should be evaluated as attempts to obtain intervention, not treated in isolation as disparagement.

    PDFDr. Sample reporting instruction; my email states I filed with DCFS and police
  9. June–August 2024

    Equipment concerns and continued complaints.

    I documented missing or relocated music equipment, including DJ turntables and stations. The August 13 communication later characterized as an improper disclosure arose in that dispute over room access and equipment. I dispute that I sent it to retaliate against Nico.

    PDFMissing studio equipment email (the "steroids" line), with Persons' Aug 12 replyPDF"The DJ stations were not in room 33 initially"; Persons: "I know where the others are"
  10. October 2–7, 2024

    Harassment complaint followed by cease-and-desist.

    On October 2, I notified Persons, Espinoza, Regional Superintendent Spicer, and Regional Director Sanford that Nico continued circling my classroom despite his reassignment. On October 7, Sanford issued a cease-and-desist directive. I contend that the directive was used to suppress reporting rather than resolve the underlying conduct.

    PDFMy report: "Nico shouldn't be circling my classroom"PDFSanford cease-and-desist directive
  11. November 19, 2024

    Professional development and EEO complaint.

    I was setting up instruments for Winter Concert rehearsals. Assistant Principal Harris placed me in the auditorium by approximately 1:35 p.m.; Persons stated that chairs and instruments were set up by approximately 1:40 p.m. Those accounts contradict the allegation that I arrived 25 minutes late with the equipment. I obviously came before the meeting started, if I brought more equipment at 1:35 p.m. or 1:40 p.m. I reported the incident to EEO investigator Melissa Espinoza that day, stating that retaliatory harassment was continuing.

  12. December 2–3, 2024

    Removed equipment and attendance allegations.

    I reported that DJ equipment was missing from Music Room 33. Ms Persons suggested that the Maintenance staff took the equipment. Sanford's review located equipment in Room 10 and Room 33, confirming that equipment was not all where I had expected it to be. I resumed instruction after the equipment was located. I also dispute the claim that I first reported to school at 1:59 p.m. on December 3; my contemporaneous response states that I arrived at 7:40 a.m.

    PDFMy response to the Dec 6 conference memo (Harris wording, 7:40 arrival, sign-in)
  13. December 3–5, 2024

    EEO reporting guidance and disposition.

    Espinoza confirmed in writing that she had directed me to report bullying and harassment under District Bulletin 5798.0. The HR/EOS process also closed. The District's full communications about the findings and reporting instructions were not included in the approximately 3,300-page disclosure I reviewed.

    PDFEspinoza directs me to report under Bulletin 5798.0PDFMy reply to the EOS closure: Joyce and Demonte were not interviewedPDFMusic Magnet thread: equipment moved, EOS closure confirmed
  14. January 21–24, 2025

    Workers' compensation report.

    After I reported becoming ill following the “removal” of an air purifier during the fires, Espinoza sent workers' compensation filing instructions on January 21. On January 24, I confirmed, "I just filed the WC claim." I contend that subsequent adverse treatment was part of the continuing retaliation, including retaliation implicated by California Labor Code section 132a.

    PDFWorkers' comp instructions, and "I just filed the WC claim"
  15. April–May 2025

    Fifth-grade student incident.

    Ms. Jackson asked me to address a student's inappropriate gesture after class. The student's own account states that he repeated another student's gesture, that Jackson had already addressed it, and that Jackson brought up the other incident to me. His account does not support the District's framing of an unsolicited conversation initiated by me.

  16. June–August 2025

    Continuing safety reports and disciplinary use of those reports.

    My communications concerning social-media contact with female students, reported inappropriate images, and unresolved safety concerns were included among the District's allegations. On August 18 I published an unlisted video describing these concerns and my treatment at Hillcrest. The fact that I made reports and statements does not establish that the underlying concerns were false.

    PDF"Groomers at an Elementary School in Los Angeles", video transcriptVIDEOGroomers at an Elementary School in Los Angeles (Aug 18, 2025)
  17. October 3, 2025

    Kindergarten instruction.

    I was instructed to teach in the general-education kindergarten classroom instead of Music Room 33, where students played Orff instruments and Ukuleles. When children asked why they could not play, I told them to inform their parents. A visiting parent spoke with me after class. This was not an interruption of music instruction.

  18. October 23, 2025

    Performance evaluation.

    Vice Principal Henderson conducted my most recent evaluation where I had the students recording original musical compositions about bullying. It is also absent from the disclosure. My earlier Hillcrest evaluation records 99% attendance, no tardies; the District supplied a much older JNLA Middle School evaluation showing 88% attendance and no tardies.

  19. January–May 2026

    Escalation and dismissal.

    The District continued investigating my communications and conduct, placed me on administrative leave on April 23, 2026, and dismissed me effective May 28, 2026. I was no longer involved in the investigation as I fled to Malaysia in January 2026 to avoid committing suicide. The loss of my dream program was devastating and I couldn’t live across the street from the students I loved.

I maintain that the discipline was the culmination of retaliation for supervision, reporting, and advocacy rather than evidence that I am unfit to teach.

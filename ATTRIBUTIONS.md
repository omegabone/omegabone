## Bundled fonts (tools/clip-renderer/public/fonts)

Rendered clips embed latin-subset webfonts, all under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1, which permits redistribution:

- **Inter** — Rasmus Andersson. https://github.com/rsms/inter
- **Cinzel** — Natanael Gama. https://github.com/NDISCOVER/Cinzel
- **EB Garamond** — Georg Duffner, Octavio Pardo. https://github.com/octaviopardo/EBGaramond12

Files are bundled rather than fetched at render time so renders are
reproducible offline; see tools/clip-renderer/README.md.

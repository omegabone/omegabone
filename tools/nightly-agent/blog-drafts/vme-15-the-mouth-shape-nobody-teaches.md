# The Mouth Shape Nobody Teaches

Ameesha's lesson is called "The Perfect Mouth Shape," and that title alone says something most singers never get told directly: your mouth is a shape, and shapes can be right or wrong before a single note leaves it. Not loud or soft. Not high or low. Shaped.

It's easy to spend years chasing bigger breath, better pitch, more confidence on stage, and never once get handed a note as basic as "this is what your mouth should actually be doing right now." That's the gap this clip sits in — the mechanical, almost boring-sounding detail that changes everything downstream once it's fixed.

This is exactly the kind of foundational fix Frequency is being built around: not another round of "sing louder" or "breathe deeper," but the specific, correctable mechanics — mouth shape, vowel placement, diction — that most singers go a whole career without anyone naming out loud. If a five-minute lesson on mouth shape can change how a line lands, imagine a full program built to catch every one of these. Keep an eye out — Frequency is coming, and this is the kind of detail it's built on.

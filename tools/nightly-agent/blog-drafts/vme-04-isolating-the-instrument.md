# Isolating the Instrument

A guitarist can tune one string without touching the other five. A singer doesn't get that luxury — every part of the instrument is attached to every other part, all the time, all at once. So how do you warm up one piece of something that never comes apart?

That's what this clip with Anna is actually about, even though it doesn't announce itself that way. Watch closely and you'll see her working one narrow slice of the voice at a time — isolating it the way you'd isolate a muscle before a lift, not because the rest of the body is irrelevant, but because you can't feel what's wrong with a part you never separate out.

This is patient work. It looks small on camera because it is small, deliberately. You're not going to see a big emotional moment here — you're going to see a person quietly finding the edges of an instrument she's had her entire life and never actually mapped. Most singers never do this. They practice the song and let the instrument stay a mystery underneath it.

Anna didn't skip that step, and you can hear the difference in everything she sings after this.

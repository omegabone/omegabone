MetaMuse is running her original song, "We Were Born From Ancient Light," ahead of campus Disco Night — and Omega spends the hour on the details that turn a nice take into a real one: no "r" on "were," a flat tongue and an open mouth so "ancient light" doesn't shrink, and a full crescendo on the word "home." "You're calling the heavens to come down," Omega tells her, "and they can't hear you if you're talking like this." By the final pass, "come back home" isn't a request anymore — it's a command.

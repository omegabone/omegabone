Chris built his musical life on bass — a decade at Berklee, a lower baritone he's never quite trusted around a microphone. Omega's fix isn't a technique, it's a reframe: stop measuring himself against his jazz-singer mother's register and start sounding like the whole grown man he actually is. By the last exercise, a raised eyebrow and a cleaner vowel are doing what years of self-doubt couldn't — putting him in tune with his own instrument instead of someone else's.

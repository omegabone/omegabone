Alejandro spent a decade in supply chain before burning out and rebuilding himself as a "happiness engineer" — and he walked into this lesson carrying an old verdict from a choir teacher: tone-deaf. Omega throws that diagnosis out. It was never his ear; it was songs picked in the wrong key for a voice that's actually a tenor, not the baritone he thought he was. Watch him color every vowel around one held "ooh" until his tone stops splitting, then rebuild his own wedding song — A Million Dreams — as a love letter to the room of dreamers in front of him.

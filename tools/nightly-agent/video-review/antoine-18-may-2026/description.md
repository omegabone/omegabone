A full-length session that moves from stagecraft basics — how to walk up, introduce yourself, and announce a song without oversharing your life story — to a genuinely moving reflection on what lasts and what fades. Omega coins a phrase mid-lesson, "storybook sound," and proves it on the spot with a quick, funny, fully personalized example. By the end it's less a vocal drill than a lesson in presence: how much, or how little, an audience actually needs to know before you sing to them.

Exercise three is all about strengthening your lower range — and it starts with a question most singers never think to ask: what actually is your instrument? Omega walks through it piece by piece, from the soft palate lift you can feel by yawning, to keeping your "ah" as bright and forward as your "e," to the buzz that finally connects your breath to the pitch. Along the way there's a homework trick for the days you can't find the air — bend over, hands overhead, and let your ribcage do the work.

Add exercise three to your daily routine, morning and evening, right alongside one and two.

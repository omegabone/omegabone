Recorded on the last day of network school in Malaysia, with the campus emptying around them, Omega and MetaMuse write a song live instead of teaching a lesson — grief, straight into the microphone. "Art will save the world," Omega says. "It has to." Line by line they build "I Thought We Had Tomorrow" — "so now, I am homeless," "like vessels can be broken, but spirit holds us close" — with Omega reminding her mid-verse that "you're not actually writing, you're downloading spirit." It closes, simply: "Make music in the center of your life."

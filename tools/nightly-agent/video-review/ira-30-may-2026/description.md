Ira runs her searching-for-home concert again, this time introducing herself under her stage name, Vega — but tonight's lesson is really about the microphone, not the melody. How close to hold it, how to keep it pointed at her mouth mid-sentence instead of letting it drift to her side, how to balance the backing track against her own voice without either one drowning the other out.

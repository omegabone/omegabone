MetaMuse walks in as Natalie the DJ and leaves with a plan to become MetaMuse the performer: Omega talks her through building a six-song live set that folds her own original tracks into a wedding and album-release DJ gig instead of just spinning other people's music behind the turntable. Between song picks they get technical — Fader, Logic, GarageBand, Suno, and the eternal problem of a track that's "already the vocal guide" with no instrumental behind it yet — before circling back to the actual instrument: a warmup built on "raise your eyebrows on the beat," scales borrowed from The Star-Spangled Banner's melody, and the reminder that none of the production matters if she skips warming up daily. "That's going to be big business," Omega tells her — and means the DJ-who-sings idea, not the software.

Grace left a career at Google for accessibility work in the arts, and by her own admission has never really been a singer — she tells Omega it's hard for her to even get on a stage. That doesn't stop Omega from putting her straight into "Roar," slowing the track down to find her real command, then stopping her mid-phrase with the actual point of the lesson: "I don't want you to sound like Katy Perry, I want you to sound like you." She finishes out of breath, admitting singing is way harder than speaking — and visibly hooked anyway.

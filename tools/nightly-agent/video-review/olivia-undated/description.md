Olivia has never taken a singing lesson in her life — by the end of this one she's decided exactly who the other lover in "Somewhere Only We Know" really is. Omega starts her on breath and the buzz (the lip-vibration drill that trips up beginners the most — "if you can do the buzz, that means you can sing"), then pushes past the notes into the actual meaning of the lyric: who she's singing to, why she's still watching, what kind of love it is. It's a first lesson that turns into real emotional work.

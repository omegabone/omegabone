Streaming in from Jakarta hours before a flight to Lombok for a hackathon, MetaMuse works through a cover ballad and admits the real problem isn't the notes — it's not knowing what to do with her hands, or her body, while she sings. Omega's fix is homework, not talk: puckered "duck lips" buzzing all day tomorrow to shrink the sound down, then a real choreography pass so her hands have somewhere to go. A short, travel-squeezed lesson that still finds the actual issue.

Orville picked Ed Sheeran's "I See Fire" because he lived through the real thing — a wildfire that burned down 60% of his hometown, and this was the song playing during the evacuation. Omega's whole lesson closes the gap between reciting a song and actually meaning it: buzz through the phrase to find your real breath marks, then let your eyes tell the room what you saw, because "his is make-believe and yours is real." It's a lesson in projecting truth into a room built for 50 people comfortably and 75 uncomfortably.

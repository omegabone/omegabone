Upanisha trained in Western classical piano and theory as a kid, but she's never had a single vocal lesson before this one. Omega takes her from the buzz straight into head voice versus chest voice — the same switch that lets Michael Jackson float from a boardroom growl into "Hi, boys and girls" — building toward the power she'll need to make Adele's "Skyfall" actually shake. Along the way they get into what makes a performance land in the first place: telling the story instead of just showing off the instrument.

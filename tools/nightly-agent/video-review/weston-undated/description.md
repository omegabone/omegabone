Weston walks in with big-guy energy and a karaoke pick of "Don't You Forget About Me," and Omega spends the session teaching him to trust his own size instead of imitating whoever he's singing along to. The turning point is a swimming-pool metaphor: buzz your lips like an Olympic runner training against water resistance, and when you finally sing, you've got power you didn't know you had. By the end he's not doing an impression of Simple Minds or Dolly Parton's "I Will Always Love You" — he's found the key where the song finally sounds like Weston.
